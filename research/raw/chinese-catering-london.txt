CATERING FAQ's
What is the minimum order for Three Uncles catering in London?
For delivery to your office or venue, the minimum order is £1,000.
For catering orders between £300 and £1,000, we offer collection from any branch.
​
Please fill in the form with your requirements.
What if I want to order catering less than £300?
For orders under £300, we suggest placing an advance order via Deliveroo with at least 24 hours’ notice.
Do you deliver catering platters to offices?
Yes. We deliver to offices across London for orders over the £1,000 minimum spend.
Do you deliver catering platters to weddings and private events?
Yes. We cater for weddings and private events for orders over the minimum spend, typically 75 people or more or £1,000 minimum spend.
Can I customise the menu?
Yes. The set menus are suggestions and can be customised.
Please contact:
[A mailto:catering@threeuncles.co.uk] catering@threeuncles.co.uk
How far in advance should I order?
Orders under £500: ideally 48 hours’ notice
Larger orders: ideally 72 hours’ notice
For urgent requests, please contact:
[A mailto:catering@threeuncles.co.uk] catering@threeuncles.co.uk
Do you offer individual meal boxes?
Yes. We offer both individual meal boxes and sharing-style catering platters.
Do you deliver outside of London?
No. We currently only deliver within London.
Are disposable plates and cutlery provided?
We provide:
Napkins
Disposable chopsticks
Disposable cutlery
If plates are required, customers should contact:
[A mailto:catering@threeuncles.co.uk] catering@threeuncles.co.uk
Can I pay via invoice?
Yes. Payment can be made via invoice or payment link.
Please contact:
[A mailto:catering@threeuncles.co.uk] catering@threeuncles.co.uk
