With locations around London, Three Uncles is a top destination for Cantonese cooking. Roasted meats are a speciality here in particular. The blue-fronted restaurant is decked out with neon signs and feels buzzy. Choose from a variety of roasted meats served with rice for a simple but satisfying meal, or go for lo mein noodles, plant-based ma po tofu, or freshly-steamed dim sum.
