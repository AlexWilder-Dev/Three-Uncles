WHERE TO FIND US IN
KINGS CROSS
Find Three Uncles at 66 York Way, around five minutes’ walk behind King’s Cross station, with St Pancras, Granary Square and Coal Drops Yard also nearby.
Our casual Chinese restaurant specialises in Cantonese roast meats: roast duck, char siu BBQ pork and crispy pork belly, all chopped to order by our chefs behind the glass counter. You can enjoy dim sum and handmade dumplings too, alongside rice dishes and lo mein egg noodles.
We’re open for lunch and dinner, with 40 seats inside (and a few outside) with beer and wine available to enjoy with your meal.

KINGS CROSS  FAQ's
Where is Three Uncles King’s Cross?
You’ll find us at 66 York Way, London N1 9AG, behind King’s Cross station and around five minutes’ walk along York Way. Granary Square, Coal Drops Yard and St Pancras station are also nearby.
Can I book a table?
We’re walk-ins only and don’t take bookings. We’re open throughout the day for lunch and dinner, just drop in.
Can I eat in or order takeaway?
Both. We have 40 seats inside, plus a few outside, or you can take your food away. Order and pay at the counter, then collect your food when we call your number. We don’t currently offer delivery from King’s Cross.
Do you offer catering for offices in King’s Cross?
Yes, we offer catering for office lunches, meetings and events in King’s Cross. Visit our
[A https://www.threeuncles.co.uk/chinese-catering-london] catering page to explore the options and send us an enquiry.
What are your opening hours?
We’re open Monday to Sunday, 11:45am–9:30pm.
Last orders are at 9:15pm.
Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?
Yes. Our mushroom mapo tofu with jasmine rice, pak choi and Hong Kong chilli oil is vegan. Vegetarian options include mushroom and pak choi bao, and pak choi lo mein with egg noodles.
Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible?
Yes. We have a step-free entrance, wheelchair access and an accessible toilet.
Do you have outdoor seating, and are dogs welcome?
We have a small outdoor seating area under a canopy, sheltered from the rain. Pet dogs are welcome outside, but not inside the restaurant. Guide dogs are welcome inside.
Do you serve alcohol?
Yes, we serve beer and wine.
